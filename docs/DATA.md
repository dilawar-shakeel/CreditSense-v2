# CreditSense v2: Data

The dataset is the Kaggle SBA national loan file (`SBAnational.csv`, "Should This Loan be Approved or Denied?"). It stays out of Git in `data/raw/`; its SHA-256 is committed in `data/raw/CHECKSUM.txt` and checked by `just verify-data`.

Every number on this page is generated by the pipeline. Do not edit the generated sections by hand; rerun the command named in each section.

## Prediction point and target

- **Prediction point:** at approval. A feature may use only information known at application or at approval (approved amount and SBA guarantee share are allowed).
- **Target:** charged off (`CHGOFF`) vs paid in full (`P I F`), among approved, disbursed loans. Loans without an outcome are excluded from training and used as the demo set; their count is reported by the pipeline.
